# Stage 2: Feature Understanding

**Purpose:** Read the PRD and Story documents, deeply understand the feature, and explain it to the developer in plain terms before any planning begins. Alignment here prevents rework in every downstream stage.

---

## Collaboration Model

**Plan first:** Before reading anything, state what you're about to analyze and what you'll produce from this stage. Get sign-off.

---

## Analysis

Read the story file and any linked PRD, design specs, or related documents referenced within it. Build a complete understanding of:

- What the feature does from the user's perspective
- Who uses it and under what conditions
- Acceptance criteria — what "done" looks like
- Business rules and constraints embedded in the requirements
- Dependencies on existing features, data, or services
- Anything ambiguous, missing, or potentially conflicting in the spec

---

## Plain-Terms Explanation

Present your understanding to the developer clearly:

1. **Feature summary** — what this feature does in 2-3 sentences, no jargon
2. **User flows** — the key actions a user can take, in order
3. **Acceptance criteria** — restated in plain terms, numbered
4. **Constraints and rules** — business logic that must be respected
5. **Open questions** — anything unclear or under-specified that needs a decision before planning

Invite the developer to correct, clarify, or add context. This is their chance to catch misalignments early.

---

## Sign-Off

Reach explicit agreement: "Does this match your understanding of the feature?" Resolve all open questions before proceeding.

---

## Sidecar Update

Update the sidecar changelog for Stage 2:
- Status: completed
- Key decisions or clarifications captured
- Open questions resolved
